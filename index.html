The studio

Independent by choice.

Wellglory LLC is a one-person app studio based in San Francisco, California. We build focused mobile tools for Android and iOS — apps that solve specific problems without asking you to subscribe to anything you shouldn’t have to.

The app store is full of fleeceware — apps that charge monthly for features that have no recurring cost to build or maintain. A QR scanner. A habit tracker. A unit converter. These don’t need subscriptions. We don’t charge them.

Every app we ship has a free tier that’s genuinely useful. If there’s a Pro version, you buy it once and own it. No renewal notices. No price hikes. No “your subscription will automatically renew” emails designed to catch you off guard.

01Pay once, own forever

Pro versions are one-time purchases. No subscriptions on features that don’t have ongoing costs.

02Free means actually free

The free tier works. It’s not crippled to push you toward a purchase. You’ll know what Pro adds before you decide.

03Private by default

We don’t build ad-supported apps. We don’t sell data. What you do in our apps stays on your device.

04Long-term support

Small apps don’t get abandoned. We update, fix bugs, and keep things working for the long haul.
